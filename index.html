Problem statement

The preliminary investigation revealed that the existing Berong SafeScape web portal faces several limitations that hinder its effectiveness as a community-based fire safety information and education platform. These limitations include performance inefficiencies associated with its legacy architecture, delays in the dissemination of fire safety information, limited accessibility for vulnerable populations and young learners, inadequate mechanisms for personalized fire safety education, and the absence of comprehensive administrative tools for monitoring and managing community engagement. As a result, the platform is unable to sustain continuous, accessible, and responsive fire safety information dissemination and educational support.

In response to these identified gaps, this study seeks to design, develop, and evaluate an enhanced SafeScape V2 platform. Specifically, it aims to answer the following research questions:

  1. How can the existing Berong SafeScape Web Portal be restructured and migrated to an optimized PHP Laravel architecture to support system performance, scalability, security, maintainability, and alignment with relevant Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) Government Web Hosting Service (GWHS) requirements?
  2. How can a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based chatbot powered by the Gemini API and grounded in BFP knowledge resources be developed, integrated, and evaluated in terms of Response Accuracy, Contextual Relevance, Response Completeness, Knowledge Retrieval Effectiveness, and Hallucination/Error Rate for automated fire safety information dissemination?
  3. How can a voice-enabled accessibility framework incorporating Web Speech API-based Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS), age-appropriate educational content, and user-centered UI/UX design be developed and evaluated in terms of usability, accessibility, ease of interaction, and user engagement among diverse user groups?
  4. How can an intelligent administrative dashboard and adaptive learning engine be developed to monitor user engagement and assessment performance, provide feedback analytics, and determine appropriate difficulty levels for module quizzes and the final examination based on learner profile and assessment performance?
  5. What is the level of evaluation of the developed Berong SafeScape Web Portal in terms of Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Reliability, Security, Maintainability, and Flexibility based on selected characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2023 as assessed by Information Technology experts; factual accuracy, contextual relevance, completeness, and operational appropriateness of the fire safety information and AI-assisted functions as assessed by Bureau of Fire Protection personnel; and usability, accessibility, ease of interaction, and overall acceptability as assessed by community end-users?

Objectives

General objective. The primary objective of this study is to design, develop, and evaluate an enhanced Berong SafeScape Web Portal for the Bureau of Fire Protection - Sta. Cruz, Laguna using an optimized PHP Laravel architecture integrated with artificial intelligence and voice-enabled accessibility technologies to provide an accessible, intelligent, secure, and user-centered fire safety education platform.

Specific objectives.

  • Backend Modernization & Architecture: To develop and implement an enhanced Berong SafeScape Web Portal by restructuring and migrating the existing portal to an optimized PHP Laravel architecture using a phased conversion approach, and to evaluate the resulting system in terms of performance, scalability, security, maintainability, and alignment with relevant DICT Government Web Hosting Service (GWHS) requirements.
  • RAG-Grounded AI Knowledge Dissemination: To develop, integrate, and evaluate a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based chatbot powered by the Gemini API and grounded in BFP knowledge resources in terms of Response Accuracy, Contextual Relevance, Response Completeness, Knowledge Retrieval Effectiveness, and Hallucination/Error Rate for automated fire safety information dissemination.
  • Voice Accessibility & User-Centered UI/UX: To design, implement, and evaluate a voice-enabled accessibility framework incorporating Web Speech API-based Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS), age-appropriate educational content, and user-centered UI/UX design in terms of usability, accessibility, ease of interaction, and user engagement among diverse user groups.
  • Adaptive Learning Engine & Admin Analytics: To develop an intelligent administrative dashboard with user engagement and assessment analytics and an adaptive learning engine using Random Forest classification to determine appropriate Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty levels for module quizzes and the final examination based on learner profile and assessment performance.
  • Tri-Partite Software Quality & Domain Evaluation: To evaluate the developed Berong SafeScape Web Portal in terms of Functional Suitability, Performance Efficiency, Reliability, Security, Maintainability, and Flexibility based on selected characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2023 as assessed by Information Technology experts; to validate the factual accuracy, contextual relevance, completeness, and operational appropriateness of the system’s fire safety information and AI-assisted functions through Bureau of Fire Protection personnel; and to determine the usability, accessibility, ease of interaction, and overall acceptability of the system as assessed by community end-users.

Methodology

The study employs a Developmental Research Design (R&D) guided by the Design Science Research Methodology (DSRM) and an Agile Iterative and Incremental Development Model across six cyclical engineering phases, validated through rigorous internal diagnostics and tri-partite stakeholder evaluation.

01

Requirements Analysis

Defined technical prerequisites for migrating legacy infrastructure to an optimized PHP Laravel 11 architecture. Formulated DICT GWHS compliance benchmarks and curated the 500-record BFP fire safety knowledge base for RAG pipeline indexing.

02

System & UI/UX Design

Architected Model-View-Controller (MVC) routing blueprints, normalized relational schemas (PostgreSQL), and dense vector embedding stores. Designed accessible, high-contrast, child-friendly user interfaces in consultation with BFP personnel.

03

Full-Stack Development

Engineered the core PHP Laravel backend with caching and database indexing. Integrated the Web Speech API to provide native Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) capabilities alongside the centralized administrative dashboard.

04

AI & Service Integration

Connected the cloud-based Google Gemini API to a weighted keyword-based RAG pipeline (500-record BFP knowledge base) for context-grounded fire responses. Deployed dual Scikit-Learn Random Forest classifiers for dynamic quiz and final examination difficulty prediction (Easy, Medium, Hard).

05

Internal Testing & Verification

Executed 85 researcher-led binary test verification cases (100% pass rate) spanning functional, security, accessibility, and AI retrieval tests. Conducted load and stress testing to ensure responsive database querying under high concurrency.

06

Deployment & Evaluation

Deployed the production-ready portal for comprehensive quantitative evaluation involving 75 stakeholders: 10 IT experts on ISO/IEC 25010:2023, 15 BFP personnel for operational/domain validation, and 50 community end-users for usability.

Phase 04: AI & Service Integration Deep Dive

Guided by the Design Science Research Methodology (DSRM) within a Developmental Research Design (R&D), Phase 04 focuses on building and validating the intelligent software artifacts of SafeScape V2. This phase orchestrates the deployment of the machine learning predictive pipeline and the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) conversational agent, seamlessly integrating them with the PHP Laravel 11 backend and Python microservices.

ML

Machine Learning Pipeline (Adaptive Learning Engine)

To provide individualized instruction, SafeScape incorporates an adaptive assessment engine driven by dual machine learning models that dynamically calibrate evaluation difficulty based on learner mastery and profile dynamics.

  • Dual Random Forest Classifiers: Deployed two independent Scikit-Learn Random Forest classification models to dynamically predict and route module quiz difficulty and comprehensive final examination difficulty across Easy, Medium, and Hard tiers.
  • 5,000-Record Synthetic Dataset & 80/20 Validation: The Random Forest models were trained, tuned, and evaluated on a structured 5,000-record synthetic student dataset modeling realistic learning behaviors, using an 80/20 train-test split to ensure high generalization accuracy and prevent model overfitting.
  • Cross-Platform Model Export & Compilation: Serialized the trained models, feature scalers, and label encoders using joblib for Python microservices. In addition, converted the decision trees into native PHP classes via m2cgen (Model to Code Generator) for high-throughput, in-memory inference directly inside the PHP Laravel 11 backend without inter-process latency.
Stack: Scikit-Learn • Random Forest • m2cgen • joblib • Laravel 11 • Python
AI

Generative AI Integration (RAG Chatbot)

To provide verified, accessible, and continuous fire safety counsel without hallucination risks, the system implements a domain-grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) conversational pipeline engineered under the DSRM framework.

  • Weighted Keyword-Based Retrieval Architecture: Built a specialized RAG retrieval mechanism that extracts the most contextually relevant fire safety information before invoking generative inference. User queries undergo automated NLP text preprocessing—including lowercase conversion, punctuation removal, and stop-word filtering—to isolate high-signal search tokens.
  • Localized 500-Record CSV BFP Knowledge Base: Preprocessed query keywords are dynamically evaluated against a curated, localized 500-record CSV knowledge base compiled from Republic Act No. 9514 (Fire Code of the Philippines), station standard operating procedures, and emergency response protocols.
  • Weighted Relevance Scoring Mechanism: Candidate records are ranked using a multi-field weighted scoring algorithm: 3 points for question matches, 2 points for category or subcategory matches, and 1 point for answer matches.
  • Google Gemini API Contextual Grounding: Up to 15 of the highest-ranking records are extracted and supplied as contextual input to the cloud-based Google Gemini API, ensuring strictly grounded, factual, and hallucination-free fire safety responses.
Stack: Google Gemini API • Weighted Keyword RAG • 500-Record BFP CSV • NLP Preprocessing • RA 9514
DSRM Developmental Alignment: The AI integration artifacts established in Phase 04 underwent iterative verification via 85 binary test cases before advancement to the tri-partite stakeholder evaluation involving IT experts, BFP firefighters, and community end-users.

System Features & Screenshots

Secure Authentication & Multi-Role Onboarding

Provides a secure gateway for citizens, young learners, and BFP station responders with role-based access control (RBAC). Features a multi-step registration wizard with demographic and barangay profiling, active session conflict detection, and single-device login protection.

  • Role-based routing tailored for Kids, Adults, Professionals, and Administrators
  • Active session conflict detection, CSRF protection, and single-device security
  • Multi-step registration wizard capturing demographic and barangay data for BFP reporting
